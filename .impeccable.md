# Design Context

## Audience
- Business owners and operations leads (PH and remote/global) who need AI to remove manual work.
- Hiring managers evaluating an AI engineer.

## Use cases
- Decide in under a minute whether Les can solve their operational problem.
- See proof (shipped systems), then book a discovery call or ask Pao.

## Brand personality
Ships fast, solves real problems, no hype. Calm, precise, operational: an engineer's console, not a sci-fi demo.

## Visual rules
- Obsidian base, a single gold signal accent (`--primary`). One accent per view.
- Type: Clash Display (display) + Satoshi (body). Mono only for real data (logs, timestamps).
- Motion explains state (a workflow running step by step), never decoration. Transform/opacity only. Respect prefers-reduced-motion.
- No invented metrics. Simulated demos are labeled as such; proof lines cite real shipped work.
